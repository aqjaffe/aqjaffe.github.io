I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in statistics at Columbia University, working under the supervision of Bodhisattva Sen. Previously, I received my PhD in statistics from UC Berkeley and my BS in mathematics from Stanford.

email: a.q.jaffe@columbia.edu


My research in mathematical statistics usually involves interactions between probability, statistics, and geometry. Some specific topics I'm interested in include: clustering, empirical Bayes, Fréchet means, information geometry, optimal transport, and Riemannian optimization.

Selected works:

  • Wasserstein-Cramér-Rao theory of unbiased estimation with Nicolás García Trillos and Bodhisattva Sen
  • Nonparametric Riemannian empirical Bayes, denoising measurements on manifolds with Leonardo Santoro and Bodhisattva Sen
  • Asymptotic theory of geometric and adaptive \(k\)-means clustering
  • Limit theorems for Fréchet mean sets with Steve Evans
